# E-Commerce-Sales-Analysis

## Pharmaceutical sales across regions 
## Overview
 This project analyzes sales performance of pharmaceutical stores across regions, product categories, and countries, in 2024 and 2025 with a strong focus on margin-driven sales.

## Tools
- Excel
- Datsa visualisation tool (Excel Dashboard).

## Dataset
- Dataset was generated from kaggle.com
- The dataset contains:
- Product category
- Name of regions and countries
- Monthly sales
- Units price


## Questions Answered
- Top 5 City by revenue
- Top Brands based on revenue generated
- Top Selling countries
- Product category sales
- Revenue change overtime

## Key Insights
- More revenue are generated in the year 2024 in the month of July
- Sales dropped in March 2025
- Wellness product generated the highest sales among the product categories
- Netherland generated the list sales among the countries.
